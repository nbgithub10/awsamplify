### Question 1
What is the main purpose of applying a polymer coating to a copper telecommunications cable?
* (A) To insulate it
* (B) To strengthen it
* (C) To increase its flexibility
* (D) To improve its conductivity
* **Answer: A**

### Question 2
What is the main purpose of the flaps on the wings of an aircraft?
* (A) To make the aircraft safer at higher speeds
* (B) To decrease lift to allow faster take-off speeds
* (C) To increase lift at lower speeds for take-off and landing
* (D) To decrease drag to allow higher take-off and landing speeds
* **Answer: C**

### Question 3
Which manufacturing method can be used to produce complex polymer components?
* (A) Sand casting
* (B) Blow moulding
* (C) Lost wax casting
* (D) Injection moulding
* **Answer: D**

### Question 4
A mass of 80 grams is lifted 200 mm by a windmill. What is the work done?
* (A) $0.16\text{ J}$
* (B) $1.60\text{ J}$
* (C) $16.0\text{ J}$
* (D) $156.8\text{ J}$
* **Answer: A**
* **Working:** $W = mgh = 0.080 \times 9.8 \times 0.2 = 0.1568 \approx 0.16\text{ J}$
